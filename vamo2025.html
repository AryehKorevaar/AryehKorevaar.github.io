VAMO 2026

VAMO 2026 will be held on April 21, 2026 from 3:15-6:45pm at Iroquois Middle School in Niskayuna, NY.

Competition Schedule and Format

Schedule:

3:15-4:00pm: Introduction and Instructions
4:00-4:30pm: Individual Round
4:45-5:35pm: Team Round
5:45-6:15pm: Pizza
6:15-6:45pm: Closing Ceremony

Individual Round:

  1. The Individual Round features 2 divisions.
    Division A: Problems geared to 8th graders, but anyone who's had experience with math outside of classes, or is looking for a challenge can also participate!
    Division B: Problems geared to 6th graders and people who are new to the VAMO. Open to all!

  2. Participants have 30 minutes to complete 15 problems.

  3. Each problem is worth 1 point, for a maximum of 15 points.

Team Round:

  1. Each team can consist of 2-4 people from any school and any divisions of the Individual Round.

  2. Teams have 50 minutes to complete 20 problems. The Team Round does not feature divisions.

  3. The last 5 minutes of the round, teams receive 3 estimation problems worth 1 point each. The 3 teams that are closest to the answer receive the point.

  4. Each problem is worth 1 point, for a maximum of 23 points.

Scoring:

Team scores are calculated by summing the average score of the team members' Individual Round with the team's Team Round score.
